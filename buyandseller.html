Use ReMarket safely


• Provide accurate product details and photos.


• Buyers and sellers are responsible for arranging the sale directly.


• Meet in a public place and inspect an item before paying.


• Never share passwords, OTPs, or sensitive financial details.


• Payment is made directly between buyer and seller. ReMarket does not process payments or arrange delivery.


This is an educational college project. Accounts, listings and messages are stored in this browser and are not secure for real transactions.
